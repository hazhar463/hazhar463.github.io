How to play

The goal: smash keeps, win crowns

Each side has two Watch Keeps (one on each lane) and one Great Keep in the middle. Knock down a Watch Keep for 1 crown. Knock down the Great Keep for all 3 crowns and an instant win.

A battle lasts 3 minutes. The player with more crowns wins. If crowns are tied, Overtime starts (1 minute): the next crown wins. Still tied? The side whose weakest keep has more health left wins.

Spark: your energy

The purple Spark bar at the bottom fills up over time, to a maximum of 10. Every card costs Spark (the number in the purple circle).

In the last minute Spark fills twice as fast, and in Overtime three times as fast. Don't sit at 10 Spark for long: you are wasting energy!

Your deck and hand

You take a deck of 8 cards into battle. You always hold 4 cards; the small card on the left is your next card. When you play a card it goes to the back of the line, so you cycle through your whole deck.

Where you can play

Troops and buildings go on your half of the rug (the side closest to you). When you knock down an enemy Watch Keep, the area just past the river in that lane opens up for you too.

Spells (Bouncy Ball, Bottle Rocket, Lullaby) can be dropped anywhere.

How troops behave

  1. After you play a troop it takes about one second to wind up, then it walks toward the nearest enemy keep, crossing the river on a bridge.
  2. On the way it fights any enemy it can see that it is able to target.
  3. Building hunters (Teddy Tank, Toy Train Ram, Balloon Bomber, Marble Golem) ignore troops and go straight for keeps and buildings.
  4. Flying troops (Paper Planes, Plush Dragon, Balloon Bomber) can only be hit by things that hit air: Kite Archers, Paper Planes, Plush Dragon, Crayon Wizard, spells and the keeps.
  5. Buildings (Block Catapult, Jack-in-the-Box, Cork Cannon, Pillow Fort) don't move. They slowly lose health over time, so place them where the enemy will walk past.

Battle rules in numbers

3:00

Battle time

Three minutes. If crowns are level, 1 minute of overtime: the first crown wins. Still level? The side whose most-damaged keep has more health left wins.

10

Spark

You start with 5 Spark, store up to 10, and gain 1 every 2.8 seconds. Double speed in the last minute, triple in overtime.

3

Keeps per side

Two Watch Keeps (1,600 HP, range 7) and a Great Keep (2,700 HP, range 7). The Great Keep wakes up when it is hit or a Watch Keep falls. Knock it down for an instant 3-crown win.

4+1

Your hand

Four cards in hand and the next one shown. Play a card and it goes to the back of the line, so your 8 cards cycle round.

2

Bridges

Ground troops cross the river on the two bridges. Fliers go straight over. Knock down a Watch Keep and you can place troops further forward on that side.

+10%

Per card level

Each card level adds 10% health and damage, up to level 11. Keeps use your deck's average level.

Controls

Drag & drop

Touch a card and drag it onto the rug. A white ring shows where it lands and how far it reaches; red means you can't place it there. Let go to play it.

Tap & tap

Tap a card to pick it up (it pops up), then tap the rug where you want it.

Pause

Tap II at the top-left to pause, or to give up a battle you can't win.

Keepsmash works in portrait and landscape, on phones and tablets. A "Battery saver" graphics option (More tab) turns off shadows for older phones.

Game modes

Crown Road

The main ladder. Win to earn trophies (+30, +33 for a 3-crown win), coins and a Toy Box. Lose and you drop up to 20 trophies, but never below the start of your current arena. Opponents are matched to your card levels, so it stays fair.

Toy Story Mode

20 story battles against the Toy King's generals, each with a twist (stronger keeps, faster enemy Spark, a full Spark start…). Earn up to 3 stars each. Works offline.

Training

Practise against the computer at Easy, Normal, Hard or Champion. No trophies won or lost: perfect for testing a new deck.

Weekly Challenge

A new challenge every week (Air Show, Rush Hour, Giant Clash, Builders' Battle, Knight Night, Toy Box Mayhem). Both sides get the same deck at level 6, so it is pure skill. Win 5 before you lose 3: coins for every win, a Toy Box at 3 wins and a big Toy Box plus 300 coins at 5.

Progress, rewards and fairness

Level up cards

Collect copies of a card plus coins to upgrade it in the Deck tab (up to level 11). Each level gives +10% health and damage. Your keeps' level is the average level of your deck.

Toy Boxes

Win battles, claim the free Toy Box every 4 hours, and collect the daily gift (a 7-day streak, with boxes on days 3 and 6). Toy Box odds per card: Common 70% · Rare 22% · Epic 7% · Legendary 1%. Only cards from arenas you have reached can drop.

Crown Road rewards

27 free rewards along the trophy road, from 25 to 3,500 trophies: coins, Toy Boxes, and a Big Toy Box at every new arena. They use your best trophies ever, so a losing streak never takes them away.

Daily quests

Three new quests every day (win battles, take crowns, cast spells…), each paying coins, plus a bonus Toy Box when you finish all three.

Shop

Three daily deals let you buy card copies with coins you earned. Optional coin packs (from $0.99, bought through Google Play) only speed things up. Every card in the game can be earned for free by playing; there are no pay-only cards.

Fair play promise

Opponents are matched to your card levels. Odds for every random reward are shown in the game. No ads during battles (just a short one between some battles, and none in your first week), no pop-up offers, and no chat with strangers. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns them off.

Achievements (+100 coins each)

First Victory · Perfect Smash (3-crown win) · First Upgrade · 10 Wins · 50 Wins · Story Complete.

Top tips

  • Defend, then counter-attack. Troops that survive your defence walk on to the enemy keep, making a free push.
  • Put tanks in front. Teddy Tank soaks up hits while Duck Cannons and Archers shoot from behind.
  • Answer fliers with Archers, Paper Planes, the Dragon or the Wizard. Knights, Ninjas and Soldiers can't hit them.
  • Pull troops to the middle. A building placed in the centre of your half lures enemy troops into range of both your keeps.
  • Save Bouncy Ball for groups (Planes, Archers, Ninjas) or to finish a weak keep.
  • Lullaby stops a big push: put the Golem and its friends to sleep while your keeps keep shooting.
  • Pillow Fort pulls tanks away from your keeps: place it in the middle of your half.
  • Watch the double-Spark minute: it's the best time for a huge push.
  • Keep your average cost around 3.5 so you can always answer what your opponent plays.

Full rollout plan

Stage 1: Build & test (Nov 2026)

Stage 2: Google Play internal test (Nov 2026)

Signed app bundle uploaded to the internal testing track; tested on real phones and tablets (portrait, landscape, low-end mode).

Stage 3: Production review (Nov 2026)

Store listing, content rating (Everyone, mild cartoon fantasy violence), data safety (no data collected), privacy policy and screenshots completed; sent to Google for review. Available in all countries Google Play supports.

Stage 4: Staged rollout (after approval)

Version 2.0 adds the live game server. It starts as an open beta in a few countries, then goes worldwide once matchmaking times and server costs are proven.

The Apple App Store launch follows the plan below, with the same save game on both systems once sign-in arrives.

Plan for Apple (iPhone & iPad)

Why it's ready for Apple

Keepsmash is made with the Godot engine, which exports native iOS apps from the same project. The battle engine, menus, toy models and sounds are shared; only store and sign-in pieces differ.

What's needed

  • Apple Developer Program membership (US$99 a year) for BriskNest US LLC (needs the company's D-U-N-S number)
  • A Mac with Xcode, or a cloud Mac build (for example GitHub Actions macOS runners) to compile and sign the app
  • App Store Connect listing, iPhone and iPad screenshots, App Privacy "nutrition label" (no data collected in v1)
  • Age rating questionnaire (4+/9+ for mild cartoon violence)

Steps

  1. Enrol BriskNest US LLC in the Apple Developer Program.
  2. Add an iOS export preset; adapt safe areas (notch, Dynamic Island) and test on iPhone SE → iPad Pro.
  3. Replace Google-only features: Game Center for leaderboards/achievements, iCloud save, StoreKit for optional coin packs.
  4. Build on macOS CI, upload to TestFlight, beta with friends and family.
  5. Submit for App Store review; release worldwide.
  6. Cross-play: iPhone and Android players meet in the same online battles from version 2.0.

Target: Q2 2027, after version 2.0 proves the online server.

FAQ

Is Keepsmash free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. All cards, arenas and modes can be earned by playing.

Do I need the internet?

No. Version 1.0 plays fully offline against computer opponents. Online battles arrive in version 2.0.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Version 1.0 uses Android's backup to your Google account: sign in with the same Google account on the new phone and restore. Play Games cloud save comes in 1.1.

Is it OK for kids?

Battles are toys bonking toys: no blood, no chat with strangers, and no ads in your first week (after that, short ads between some battles, never during one). A dedicated Wonder Squad kids' edition is planned.
