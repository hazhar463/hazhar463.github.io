Keepsmash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to Keepsmash version 1.x for Android (package com.brisknest.keepsmash), published by BriskNest US LLC (Hassan Azhar).

The short version

We (BriskNest) do not run our own servers for Keepsmash and do not collect your personal data ourselves. The game uses three Google services that do process some data: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads, Google Play Games for the optional sign-in, cloud save and trophy leaderboard, and Google Play Billing for optional purchases. Each is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Keepsmash is free and shows ads: no ads at all in your first 7 days, then a short ad between some battles (never during one, never in the tutorial), plus optional reward videos that you choose to watch for a bonus. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: where the law requires it (for example the EU, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice any time with More › Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads between battles for good.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in with Play Games, Google shares your Play Games player name and player ID with the game so it can save your progress to your Google account (cloud save) and post your best trophy count to the public "Trophies" leaderboard. You can sign out or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Google Play Games settings.

Optional purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are optional and are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see or store your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a product was bought so it can deliver it.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (cards, levels, coins, trophies, settings and the player name you type) is saved on your device, and, if you signed in with Play Games, also in your own Google account's saved games. Android backup may also copy the save to your Google account. We cannot see these copies.

Permissions

The app uses internet access (ads, Play Games, purchases), the advertising ID (ads), vibration (short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings) and Google Play billing. It does not use location services, camera, microphone or contacts.

Links

The "How to play", "Privacy policy" and "Send feedback" buttons open your web browser or email app. Anything you choose to send us by email is used only to answer you and improve the game.

Children

Keepsmash is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Future versions

Planned features such as online battles may need more data. This policy will be updated, and the Google Play data-safety section changed, before any such version is released.
