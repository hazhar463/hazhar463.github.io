Isle of Varan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Isle of Varan ("the app") is a board game made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device: your chosen player name, avatar and colour, coins, lives, levels, campaign stars, awards, game statistics, settings, and any game in progress. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

The app also offers an optional "transfer code" that you can copy and paste yourself to move progress to another device. The code is only shared if you choose to share it.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (coins and life refills) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as online play, we will update this policy and the date above before those features are released.

Contact

Questions? Contact us through www.hazhar.com.
