Snip Snack: Rope Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Snip Snack: Rope Puzzle ("the app") is a game made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your data to our servers. Its only network use is Google Play Billing, for optional purchases.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device: stars, levels, coins, lives, hints, hats and candy skins, daily-gift streak, achievements and settings. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a starter pack and infinite lives) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as ads or online play, we will update this policy and the date above before those features are released.
