Wonder Squad Kids Apps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Wonder Squad learning apps for young children made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"): Wonder ABC, Wonder 123, Wonder Shapes, Wonder Words and Wonder Math ("the apps").

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information. The apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no links out of the app.

Information stored on the device

Each app saves the stars, coins and chosen colours on the device so progress is kept between sessions. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Coins and in-app purchases

Every activity in the apps is free to play. Children earn coins by playing, and coins can be spent on optional crayon colours and backgrounds. A grown-up can also buy coin packs; every purchase sits behind a grown-ups-only question and is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store payment details. Google Play's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Voice

The apps read letters, numbers and words aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example, Speech Services by Google). Only the app's own learning text is passed to that engine; nothing your child does is recorded, and the apps never use the microphone or camera.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for the device's Google account, Android may copy the saved stars to that account's backup so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under the account's settings; we cannot see it.

Children

The apps are designed for children aged about 3 to 6 and are made to follow Google Play's Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to store, review or delete.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that handles information, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions from parents are welcome through www.hazhar.com.
