Jet Set Junior — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Jet Set Junior ("the app") is a travel-the-world board game for children, made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest under the Wonder Squad brand ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or share any information at all, from children or adults. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet.

Information stored on the device

The app saves game progress on the device only: player names typed into the game, Stars, Lucky Dice, unlocked tokens, boards and dice, achievements, settings and any game in progress. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Read-aloud

The "read aloud" button uses the device's built-in text-to-speech to speak city facts and cards. The app does not record or listen to anything.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for the family's Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to that account's backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is handled by Google under the account's settings; we cannot see it.

Changes

If a future version changes how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the date above before release.

Contact

Parents with questions can contact us through www.hazhar.com.
