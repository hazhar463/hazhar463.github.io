Break rows. Chain combos. Shake the board!

How to play

Every turn you get three pieces. Place them, break lines, and keep the board open. There is no timer and no rotating: plan ahead!

1

Drag a piece

Pick up one of the three pieces in the tray and drop it anywhere it fits on the 8×8 board. A glowing preview shows where it lands.

2

Fill a line

Complete a full row or column and it shatters. The preview lights up every line you are about to clear.

3

Chain combos

Clear lines move after move to grow your combo streak. Your streak survives two moves without a clear — then it resets.

4

Don't run out of room

When none of your pieces fit anywhere, the run ends. Use the Hold slot, boosters or an Aftershock to keep going.

Combo streak

Each move that clears at least one line adds 1 to your streak, and every line bonus is multiplied by it. A ×5 streak turns a 2-line clear from 25 into 125 points! Miss three moves in a row and the streak drops back to zero.

Coins

Every game pays coins (your score ÷ 20). You also earn coins from Adventure wins and stars, Daily Missions, Trophies, Weekly Event prizes and the 7-day Daily Gift.

Special gems & the Hold slot

Rainbow gem

Sometimes a piece carries a spinning rainbow gem. When it breaks, every gem of the board's most common colour breaks with it.

Frozen block

Found in Adventure. The first line clear only cracks the ice — the block stays. Clear its line again to break it.

📥

Hold slot

Drag a piece onto HOLD to keep it for later (one swap per new set of pieces). Drag the held piece onto the board any time.

Everyone starts with 2 Undos, 2 Bombs and 2 Rerolls. Aftershock (300 coins or a video): when you are out of room, it breaks the two fullest rows and two fullest columns so you can keep playing — once per game.

Tips from the team

Keep a 3×3 hole

The big 3×3 square and long 1×5 bars are the pieces that end runs. Always leave space for them.

Build along the edges

Fill from the walls inward. Pieces placed in the middle split the board into awkward pockets.

Use Hold early

Park the big piece in HOLD while the board is open, and bring it back when it can finish two lines at once.

Game modes

All modes are free to play, forever. Coins and boosters are optional helpers.

🗺️ Adventure — 120 levels

Six worlds: Crystal Caves, Ember Ridge, Frost Hollow, Desert Mirage, Lantern Bazaar and Starfall Peak. Each level has a goal and a move limit, and earns 1–3 stars for how quickly you finish.

  • Score — reach the target score
  • Lines — clear a number of lines
  • Gems — break gems of one colour
  • Ice — crack every frozen block

Out of moves? Buy +5 moves or watch a video. Every level has been played and beaten by our test solver.

♾️ Classic

The endless mode: play until no piece fits and chase your best score. Your game saves itself, so you can always continue later.

📅 Daily Quake

One puzzle a day, with the same pieces for every player in the world. Compare your score with friends and on the daily leaderboard.

🏆 Weekly Event

A new themed board every week (Crystal, Ember, Frost, Lantern, Starfall and Bloom weeks). Score as much as you can in 40 moves to win three prize tiers of coins and boosters.

The six Adventure worlds

Adventure has 120 levels, 20 in each world. Every level has one goal (score, lines, gems of one colour, or cracking ice), a move limit, and up to 3 stars. Each level was played and beaten by the game's own solver before release, so none of them are impossible.

Levels 1–20

💎 Crystal Caves

Where it all starts. Gentle boards that teach the four goals one at a time, with plenty of room to move.

Levels 21–40

🔥 Ember Ridge

Busier starting boards and rainbow gems twice as often, so big colour-clearing combos start to matter.

Levels 41–60

❄️ Frost Hollow

The ice world. Most levels ask you to crack every frozen block: the first line clear only cracks the ice, the second breaks it.

Levels 61–80

🏜️ Desert Mirage

A mix of every goal on fuller boards. Frozen blocks start showing up in levels that aren't about ice, too.

Levels 81–100

🏮 Lantern Bazaar

Tight boards and tighter move limits. Planning two lines ahead and using the Hold slot pays off here.

Levels 101–120

🌌 Starfall Peak

The summit: the fullest starting boards in the game. Finishing all 120 levels earns a 1,000-coin trophy.

Stars: finish quickly for 3 stars, a bit slower for 2, and anywhere inside the move limit for 1. Ran out of moves? Add 5 more for 150 coins or a short video.

Easy to learn, made for everyone

A one-minute guided tutorial

The first time you tap Play, four short lessons show you what to do with a glowing target and a moving hand: fill a row, fill a column, park a piece in the Hold slot, then use it to clear two lines at once. Already know block puzzles? Tap Skip. You can replay it any time from Settings › How to play.

Colour-blind mode

Turn on Symbols in Settings and every gem colour also gets its own shape (● ▲ ■ ◆ ★ ✚ ⬢), so you never need to tell red from green to plan a move. It works with every block style.

Four languages, right-to-left included

Tilequake is fully translated into English, Arabic (العربية), Urdu (اردو) and Spanish (Español). Arabic and Urdu screens read right to left. The game follows your phone's language, and you can switch in Settings › Language.

Stuck? Ask for a hint

You get 5 free hints every day. If you haven't moved for 10 seconds, the best spot for a piece gently glows on its own.

Plan for iPhone & iPad

Tilequake's game rules already live in a separate, platform-free "core", so the iPhone version shares the same levels, scoring and saves. Target: Version 4, May–June 2027.

1. Accounts & setup (Jan 2027)

  • Join the Apple Developer Program ($99 a year) as BriskNest US LLC — needs the company's D-U-N-S number (free, takes up to 2 weeks). Until then we can enrol as an individual.
  • App Store Connect app: "Tilequake: Block Puzzle", bundle id com.brisknest.tilequake.
  • A Mac for building: a Mac mini, or macOS build machines on GitHub Actions (they use 10× the minutes, so a Mac mini is cheaper long-term).

2. Technology (Feb–Apr 2027)

  • Convert the game core from Java to Kotlin Multiplatform, so one shared code base runs on Android and iPhone.
  • iPhone screen layer in Swift drawing with Apple's graphics (the game only needs ~15 drawing commands).
  • StoreKit 2 for coin packs, Remove Ads and the Pass; Game Center for leaderboards and achievements; iCloud for saves.
  • Google AdMob for iOS with Apple's tracking-permission prompt (App Tracking Transparency).

3. Apple's rules we design for

  • Privacy "nutrition label" and the in-app account-free design.
  • A visible Restore Purchases button (already in Settings).
  • Clear subscription terms on the Pass screen.
  • Support for every iPhone and iPad size, Dynamic Island and landscape.

4. Launch (May–Jun 2027)

  • TestFlight beta with friends and family (2–3 weeks).
  • App Store review (usually 1–3 days).
  • Phased release over 7 days, the same health checks as Android.
  • Optional cross-progress with Android through a BriskNest account.

About the game

Tilequake is an original gem block puzzle. Players drag pieces onto an 8×8 board and break full rows and columns, building combo streaks that shake the board. It has 120 Adventure levels across six worlds, a Daily Quake puzzle shared by every player, a weekly themed event, daily missions and 20 trophies, and it plays fully offline in four languages.

FAQ

Is Tilequake free?

Yes. Every mode and every level can be played for free. Coins, boosters, styles, Remove Ads and the Pass are optional.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can play every mode without internet. Leaderboards, cloud save and videos need a connection.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

No. Your progress is backed up to your Google account, and with Google Play Games it is also saved to the cloud and merged automatically.

How do I remove ads?

There are no ads in your first 7 days. After that you can buy Remove Ads once ($2.99) or join the Tilequake Pass. Both also restore on your other Android phones.

Which languages are supported?

English, Arabic, Urdu and Spanish. Tilequake follows your phone's language; you can change it in Settings.

Is it suitable for children?

Tilequake is made for players 13 and older. A kids' edition without ads (Wonder Squad Blocks) is on the roadmap.

What's the difference between Classic and Adventure?

Classic is endless: play until no piece fits and chase your best score. Adventure has 120 levels, each with a goal, a move limit and up to 3 stars.

Can I rotate pieces?

No. Pieces always keep their shape. That's part of the challenge, and why the Hold slot and Reroll booster exist.

What happens when no piece fits?

The run ends. In Classic you can use an Aftershock second chance once (300 coins or a short video), which breaks 2 rows and 2 columns so you can keep going.

Is the Daily Quake really the same for everyone?

Yes. Everyone in the world gets the same pieces in the same order that day, so scores are fair to compare on the leaderboard.

Do I need a Google account?

No. You can play everything without signing in. Signing in to Google Play Games adds leaderboards, achievements and cloud save.

Does the Pass renew automatically?

Yes, every month, until you cancel in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. You keep the Pass until the end of the month you paid for.

Will there be new levels?

Yes. Version 2 (early 2027) adds levels 121–240 and two new worlds. See the roadmap above.

What is the Winter Festival?

From 1 December to 7 January the game snows, and the Weekly Event becomes the Winter Festival with a frozen snowflake board. Reach the top prize to unlock the Winter block style for free.

How do play streaks work?

Finish at least one game a day to grow your streak. Every 7th day in a row gives you 300 bonus coins. Miss a day and the streak starts again.
