Tilequake: Block Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Tilequake is published by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect or store any personal information on our servers, and the game has no sign-in.

Advertising

Tilequake shows ads from Google AdMob (none during your first 7 days, and none at all if you buy "Remove Ads"). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how ads are viewed, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time with the "i" button on the home screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google stores your scores (leaderboards), achievements and a copy of your game progress (cloud save) in your Google Play Games account so it can be restored on another phone. This is handled by Google under its privacy policy; we do not receive your personal details.

Game progress

Your scores, coins and settings are saved only on your device. If you turn on Android backup, Android may back them up to your own Google account so they come back on a new phone. We cannot see this data.

Purchases

Optional coin packs, Remove Ads and the Tilequake Pass subscription are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Tilequake is made for players aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
