Prism Pour: Color Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Publisher: Hassan Azhar / BriskNest (hassan.azhar@brisknest.us).

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is saved on your device. Android Auto Backup may back up this save with your Google account so it returns on a new phone. We do not run our own servers and do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

Advertising

After the first 7 days of play the game shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between levels and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the full-screen ads permanently.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played so we can improve it — for example how many people play each day, how long sessions last, which levels are hard, which boosters are used and which ad campaigns bring players. Firebase collects an app-instance ID, device information (model, Android version, language), approximate location derived from IP address, and in-game events such as starting or finishing a level. It does not receive your name, email or contacts. Where consent is required (EEA/UK), ad-related measurement follows the choices you make in Google's consent form. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID and scores (levels completed, best Rush score) are sent to Google Play Games to show the leaderboards, under Google's privacy policy.

Reports and feedback

"Report a problem" in the game opens your own email app with a message to us. It includes what you write plus the game version, level, progress and phone model, so we can fix the problem. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. We use these emails only to answer you and fix the game.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Contact and deletion

Email hassan.azhar@brisknest.us with questions. Uninstalling the game removes the local save; you can delete Android backups in your Google account settings.
