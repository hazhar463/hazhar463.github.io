Rollionaire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Rollionaire is a game by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest US LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information.

What we collect

Version 1 collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Backups

Android may back up the game's save file to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup) so it can be restored on a new phone. We cannot see these backups.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game keeps a list of your purchases on your device so it can show your purchase history and your optional monthly spending limit.

Children

Rollionaire is made for players aged 13 and over.

Future versions

When online play (sign-in, friends, trading) is added, this policy will be updated before release to explain exactly what is stored and why.
