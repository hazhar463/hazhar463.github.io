Life in Harvest Valley

Ten things you'll do on your farm, from your first wheat field to the county fair.

Fields

Swipe to harvest

Drag your finger across your fields and every ripe crop jumps into your barn. Swipe across empty plots and they replant with your last crop. Wheat is ready in a couple of minutes; pumpkins and cotton take longer and are worth more.

  • 9 crops: wheat, corn, carrots, sugarcane, strawberries, pumpkins, tomatoes, cotton, sunflowers
  • Each harvest gives 2; Super Fertilizer makes it 4
  • Rain in spring, summer and autumn makes crops planted now grow 10% faster
Animals

Raise happy animals

Feed your animals with feed from the Feed Mill and collect what they make. Every pen can grow as you level up, and the Sunflower Meadow brings pigs, goats and bees.

  • Chickens lay eggs, cows give milk, sheep give wool
  • Pigs find truffles, goats give goat milk, bees make honey
  • Feed them again right away to keep the farm busy
Workshops

Bake, churn and press

Turn crops and animal goods into treats worth far more. Twelve workshops, each with its own recipes, and from level 12 you can open a second production line so two things cook at once.

  • Feed Mill, Bakery, Dairy, Sugar Mill, Smokehouse, Loom
  • Flour Mill, Juice Press, Sauce Kitchen, Jam Kitchen, Ice Cream Stand and the monthly Festival Stall
  • Bread, cakes, muffins, pizza, cheese, jams, ice cream, juices, sauces, scarves and more
Truck orders

Fill orders for coins and XP

The order board always has six requests. Load the truck, send it, and earn coins and experience. Rewards are always worth more than the goods, and you can swap any order you don't like.

  • Six orders at a time; a new one arrives soon after each delivery
  • Bigger orders unlock as you level up
  • Watch an optional video to double your last delivery
Stand and visitors

Sell at your own price

Put goods on your roadside stand at the price you choose; villagers walk by and buy. Visitors also come right onto your farm and pay more than the truck for what they need.

  • Fair price or lower: almost everyone buys
  • Price it high and fewer villagers will buy
  • Visitors pay extra coins and bonus XP
River Barge

Pack the barge, pick the trip

The River Barge docks with six crates to fill. Choose an easy, normal or long trip: longer trips ask for more but pay up to 2.2 times the value. Fill all six for a bonus, and friends can help you pack.

  • Easy, normal and long trips
  • Full barge bonus: extra coins and a Super Fertilizer
  • Fishing spot nearby: catch fish for the Smokehouse
Friends

Visit friends and help out

Pick a nickname and share your 6-letter farm code. Visit friends, read the name on their farm sign, pack a crate on their barge, bring their withered trees back to life, and send gifts and stickers.

  • Up to 3 gifts a day, stickers any time
  • Help a friend's barge: you both get rewards
  • Only your nickname is shared: no email, no real name, no chat
Seasons

A farm that changes with the year

Harvest Valley follows the real calendar. Spring blossoms, summer sun, autumn leaves and winter snow change how your farm looks, and seasonal decorations appear in the shop.

  • Spring tulips, beach umbrellas, pumpkin lanterns, snowmen
  • Rain makes crops grow 10% faster; snow in winter
  • Decorate anywhere: move decor to the spot you like
Events

County Fair, festivals and the Farm Pass

Every week the County Fair sets new tasks and a top-farm board. On the 1st of each month a two-week festival opens the Festival Stall with special candy and prizes. Daily tasks and the 12-step Farm Pass add rewards every day.

  • Weekly County Fair with a board of top farms
  • Monthly festival with a decoration as the top prize
  • Farm Pass: a free row for everyone, plus an optional gold row with the Golden Rooster and Flower Arch
Surprises

Critters and treasures

Little visitors wander across your farm: a hedgehog, a duckling, a squirrel and a butterfly. Tap them for a gift. Critters, fishing and your pet sometimes find treasures; finish a set for a prize.

  • Two treasure sets: Old Farm Things and River Finds
  • Spare pieces swap for coins
  • Your pet digs up surprises after a treat

A farm you can explore

Drag to look around, pinch to zoom. Your whole farm is one living picture.

How to play

Everything you need to know, from your first wheat to a farm full of friends.

1 Plant and harvest

  • Tap an empty plot and choose a seed. Planting uses 1 crop from your barn (or a few coins if you have none).
  • When a crop shines and sways, it's ready. Tap it to harvest. Each harvest gives 2.
  • Swipe across plots to harvest many at once. Swipe across empty plots to plant your last crop.
  • Harvest all and Replant buttons above the field do the whole field in one tap.
🌧️ When it rains, crops you plant grow 10% faster. Check the weather button at the top.

2 Raise animals

  • Chickens lay eggs, cows give milk, sheep give wool. Later: pigs find truffles, goats give goat milk and bees make honey.
  • Animals need feed made at the Feed Mill (bees love sunflowers).
  • A thought bubble shows what each animal needs: feed, or a ready product to collect. Feed + collect does the whole pen.
  • Buy more animals with the +1 button on each pen (up to 6).

3 Make goods in workshops

  • Build workshops as you level up: Feed Mill, Bakery, Smokehouse, Dairy, Sugar Mill, Loom, Flour Mill, Juice Press, Sauce Kitchen, Jam Kitchen and Ice Cream Stand.
  • Tap a workshop, pick a recipe and it starts cooking. Goods worth more than their ingredients.
  • Add queue slots, and later a second production line so it makes two things at once.
  • Changed your mind? Cancel a job and get the ingredients back.

4 Sell and earn

  • Truck orders: the board shows 6 orders. Fill one to earn coins and XP; ✕ swaps an order you don't like.
  • Roadside stand: set your own price. Villagers walk by and buy. Tap the coin bag to collect.
  • Farm visitors: villagers walk onto your farm asking for 1–2 things and pay extra.
  • River Barge: pick an easy, normal or long trip, then pack six crates before it sails for a big bonus.

5 Grow your farm

  • Earn XP to level up. New crops, animals and buildings appear when you reach their level (not before).
  • Buy more field land, then whole new areas: Orchard Hill, Sunflower Meadow, Riverside and Golden Fields.
  • Upgrade the barn when it gets full. Boosters never take barn space.
  • Decorate! Buy decorations in the Decor Album and place them anywhere: tap one, then Move.

6 Every day

  • Daily gift: visit 7 days in a row for bigger gifts.
  • Daily tasks: three small goals each day, plus a bonus when all three are done.
  • Farm Pass: tasks, orders, crates and visitors earn pass points that open 12 rewards each month.
  • Critters: a hedgehog, duckling, squirrel or butterfly sometimes wanders by. Tap it for a little gift.
  • Treasures: critters, fishing and your pet dig up treasures. Finish a set for a prize.

7 Weekly and monthly fun

  • County Fair: new tasks every week; points open prize tiers, and the weekly board shows the top farms.
  • Monthly festival: days 1–14 each month. Make Festival Candy at the Festival Stall for prizes, including a seasonal decoration.
  • Seasons: the farm changes with the real season, with rain, snow and seasonal decorations.

8 Helpers, pets and fishing

  • Juniper, your farmhand, harvests, collects and replants for 30 minutes, even while you're away.
  • Adopt a puppy, kitten or bunny and give it a name. Treat it to an egg and it digs up surprises.
  • Go fishing at the pond: tap Reel when the line is in the green. Watch for the golden fish!

A day on your farm

Play for two minutes or two hours. Here's how a typical day goes.

Morning
Collect your daily gift, check the three daily tasks, and swipe across the field to harvest what grew overnight.
Quick break
Feed the animals, start bread and cheese in the workshops, and send a truck order.
Lunch
Restock your roadside stand, help a visitor, and pack a crate or two on the River Barge.
Evening
Visit a friend's farm, send a gift, work on the County Fair tasks and claim Farm Pass rewards.
Away
Your farm keeps growing. When you come back, a "While you were away" card lets you collect everything at once.

Beginner's guide

Tips to help your farm grow faster.

1Keep the field busy

Never leave plots empty. Swipe to replant right after you harvest, and keep quick crops (wheat, corn) going while slower ones grow.

2Feed first, then cook

The Feed Mill keeps every animal producing. Make feed in batches before you start bakery or dairy recipes.

3Watch your barn

The barn has limited space. Sell extras at the stand or on the truck before it fills up, and upgrade it when you can. Boosters never take barn space.

4Price to sell

At the fair price or lower, villagers almost always buy. Raise prices for rare goods only.

5Pick the right barge trip

Short on time? Take the easy trip. Full barn? The long trip pays 2.2× and adds an Instant Grow.

6Name your farm

After your first level-up you can name your farm. It goes on the sign by the farmhouse, and friends see it when they visit. Change it any time in Settings.

7Take a farm photo

Tap 📷 to hide every button, frame your farm with pinch and drag, and save or share the picture.

8Play your way

Turn on "All labels" if you like to see everything, or keep the farm clean. Choose your language, music and sounds in Settings.

Play with friends

No email and no real name: just a nickname and a 6-letter farm code.

👫 Visit farms

Open More → Friends, pick a nickname and share your farm code. Type a friend's code to visit their farm.

⛵ Help their barge

Pack a crate on a friend's River Barge with your own goods. You earn coins and XP, and their crate is packed.

🌳 Save their trees

When a friend's tree is picked dry, they can ask for help. Bring it back to life for free on your visit.

🎁 Gifts and stickers

Send up to 3 gifts a day from your barn, and leave a sticker so they know you came by.

Free to play, fair to play

🌱 Everything is earnable

Every crop, animal, building and area can be earned by playing. Coins and boosters can also be bought, but you never need to.

📺 Ads only when you choose

No ads in the first 7 days. After that, a short ad may show now and then between deliveries. Optional videos give rewards. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase.

🎟️ Farm Pass

The free row of rewards is for everyone. A gold row with extra rewards and two special decorations can be added each month.

Questions

Is Harvest Valley free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. Optional purchases (coins, boosters, Remove Ads, Farm Pass gold row) are extras.

Does my farm keep growing while I'm away?

Yes. Crops grow, workshops finish and villagers shop at your stand. When you come back, the "Welcome back" card shows everything that's ready and can collect it all with one tap.

How do I keep my farm on a new phone?

Sign in with Google Play Games (Settings). Your farm is saved to your Google account and comes back on a new phone.

Do I need the internet?

No. You can farm offline. Friends, the weekly board and cloud saves need a connection.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. By default the game uses your phone's language.

Too many labels on the farm?

By default the farm only shows labels for things that need you. Turn on Settings → All labels to see every timer.

Who can see my farm?

Only if you join Friends: players who know your 6-letter farm code can visit. You can take your farm offline any time in Friends. Read the privacy policy.
