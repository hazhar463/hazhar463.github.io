Privacy policy

Harvest Valley: Farm Story (Android app com.brisknest.harvestvalley), made by BriskNest US LLC. Last updated 16 November 2026.

The short version

You can play Harvest Valley without giving us any personal information. We don't ask for your name, email or location, and we don't sell data. Some optional features use Google services, described below.

What the game stores on your phone

Your farm (crops, coins, levels and settings) is saved on your phone so you can keep playing. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Optional features and the services they use

FeatureServiceWhat is shared
Cloud save (sign in with Google Play Games)Google Play GamesYour farm save, stored in your Google account so it comes back on a new phone. Covered by Google's privacy policy.
Friends and the weekly fair board (only if you choose a nickname)Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore)An anonymous player ID, the nickname you choose, your 6-letter farm code, your level, your farm save (so friends can visit), your weekly fair points, and the help, gifts and stickers you send. No email or real name. "Take my farm offline" in Friends deletes your farm and score.
Ads and reward videosGoogle AdMob and Google's consent tool (UMP)AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, as you allow in the consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between play.
PurchasesGoogle Play BillingPayments are handled by Google Play. We only receive confirmation of what was bought.
Ready reminders (optional)Android notifications on your phoneNothing leaves your phone. Turn them off in Settings.

Children

Harvest Valley is meant for players aged 13 and over.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in and without Friends: nothing about you is stored outside your phone except what ads use under your consent choice.
  • Delete your online farm: Friends → "Take my farm offline".
  • Ask us to delete anything else: email us (below) with your farm code.
