Wonder Squad Playhouse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Wonder Squad Playhouse games for children made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"): Tiny Chef Rush, Bridge Buddies, Panda Picnic Café, Toon Turbo Town, Birdie Bubble Town, Shelly's Sea Quest, Cozy Critter Island, Toy Factory Tycoon, Brain Buddies Gym and Word Wizard Puzzles ("the games").

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information. The games have no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no chat and no links out of the game.

Information stored on the device

Each game saves progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades and decorations) on the device. Android's own backup can copy this to the family's Google account so progress returns on a new phone. We cannot see this data. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Voice

Some games read words aloud using the text-to-speech engine already on the device. No audio is recorded.

Coins and in-app purchases

Every level and mode is free to play, and children earn coins by playing. A grown-up can also buy optional coin packs. Every purchase sits behind a grown-ups-only question and is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store payment details. Google Play's privacy policy applies to purchases: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The games are designed for children and follow Google Play's Families policy. They are suitable for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
