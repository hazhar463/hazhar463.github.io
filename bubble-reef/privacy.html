Bubble Reef Rescue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 (adds save codes, crash reports, game statistics and the daily reminder). Publisher: BriskNest US LLC (Hassan Azhar). Contact: hassan.azhar@brisknest.us

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters and collection) is saved on your device. Android's backup may copy it to your Google account.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile name and a saved game with your progress are stored by Google, so your progress can be restored on another device. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Save codes

If you choose Settings → Move to another phone → Make a save code, a copy of your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, collection and settings) is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, United States) under a random 10-letter code, linked to an anonymous ID created for your device. No name, email or other personal information is included. Anyone with the code can load that progress, so keep it private. To delete a save code, email us the code.

Crash reports and game statistics

To fix bugs and balance levels, the game uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. When the game crashes it sends a crash report (device model, Android version, app version, the screen you were on and technical details of the error). While you play it records gameplay events such as starting, winning, losing or leaving a level (level number, stars, shots used) and which in-app products are bought. These come with a Firebase installation ID and device information; they don't include your name or contact details and are not used for advertising by us. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Daily reminder

If you turn on Settings → Daily reminder, the game shows one local notification a day when your daily gift or Reef Streak is waiting. It is scheduled on your phone; no data is sent for it. You can turn it off at any time.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a purchase confirmation (product and order ID) so we can deliver the item, but never your payment details.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward videos you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never during play, none in your first 7 days, and none at all after the one-time Remove Ads purchase). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location and app interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deletion

You can play without signing in, without making a save code and with the reminder off. Uninstalling the game removes the progress stored on your phone. To ask us to delete a save code or any other data we hold, email the address below; we answer within 30 days. Firebase keeps crash reports for up to 90 days and detailed game statistics for 2 to 14 months (summary reports may be kept longer).
