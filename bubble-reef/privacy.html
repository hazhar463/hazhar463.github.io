Bubble Reef Rescue — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026. Publisher: BriskNest US LLC (Hassan Azhar). Contact: hassan.azhar@brisknest.us

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters and collection) is saved on your device. Android's backup may copy it to your Google account.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile name and a saved game with your progress are stored by Google, so your progress can be restored on another device. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location and app interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
