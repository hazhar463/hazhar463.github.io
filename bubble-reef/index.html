How to play

🎯 Aiming and shooting

Drag anywhere to aim, then let go to shoot. The guide line shows the path. Aim at a side wall and the bubble bounces, which is how you reach bubbles hiding behind others.

Your next bubble waits beside Otto. Tap Otto (or the next bubble) to swap them.

💥 Popping and dropping

Join 3 or more bubbles of one colour to pop them. Any bubbles left hanging with nothing holding them up fall off the reef. Drops score more than pops, and falling friends are freed too.

The biggest shots cut a reef off near the top so a whole branch tumbles down.

🔢 Shots

Each level gives you a set number of shots (shown at the bottom). The counter turns red when 5 are left. Run out before the goal is done and you can try again, or keep going with +5 shots for coins or a short optional video.

⭐ Stars

Win with shots to spare for more stars: about a quarter of your shots left earns 3 stars, about a tenth earns 2 stars, and any win earns 1 star. A level won after buying extra shots gives 1 star. Replay any level to improve.

Tips from Otto

  • Drop beats pop. Aim for the bubbles that hold up big branches; everything underneath falls and scores more.
  • Use the walls. A bounce can sneak a bubble into a gap you can't reach straight on.
  • Swap before you shoot. If the colour in the shooter is no use, tap Otto to use the next one instead.
  • Save the Fire Bubble for rocks, ice and thick reefs, and the Rainbow Bubble for spots where several colours meet.
  • Keep shots spare for 3 stars: finish with about a quarter of your shots left.
  • Watch the jellyfish and clams: wait a shot or two for the right colour or an open clam.
  • Protect your streak. Use your free streak bubbles early on hard levels.
  • Stuck? Replay an earlier level for stars (they open chests) or try Endless mode for coins.

Questions

Do I need the internet to play?

No. The whole game works offline. You only need a connection for cloud save, leaderboards, optional videos and purchases.

I'm stuck on a level. What can I do?

Use a booster, keep going with +5 shots, or replay earlier levels for stars and chest rewards. Your Reef Streak also gives free bubbles after a few wins.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Open Settings and sign in with Google Play Games on the old phone, then sign in with the same account on the new one. Progress from both phones is combined, so you never lose stars.

I bought Remove Ads. Why can I still see a video?

Remove Ads stops every ad that plays on its own. The only videos left are the optional ones you choose to watch for a reward, like "Double your coins".

A purchase didn't arrive.

Close and reopen the game while online. Purchases are checked again at every start and delivered once Google Play confirms them. Pending payments arrive when they clear.

Yes, that's the plan for version 2.0. Watch this page for news.
