Honey Hollow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Honey Hollow ("the app") is a match-3 puzzle game made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest US LLC ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device: levels reached, stars, best scores, coins, boosters, daily-gift streak and settings. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Online leaderboard (optional)

If you choose to join the "Top Bees" leaderboard, the app sends the following to our leaderboard database (Google Firebase, operated by Google on our behalf):

  • the nickname you type in (please don't use your real name),
  • your highest level reached and total stars,
  • a random, anonymous player ID created by Firebase Authentication so only your device can update your entry.

Your nickname, level and stars are shown to other players on the leaderboard. We do not collect your email, real name, contacts, location or device identifiers for advertising, and we do not sell or share this information with anyone else. It is used only to show the leaderboard.

You can delete your leaderboard entry at any time in the app: open the leaderboard and tap "Remove me from the leaderboard". You can also ask us to delete it through the contact link below. If you never join, nothing is sent.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (coins and boosters) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Ads and analytics

The app shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will update this policy and the date above before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests? Contact us through www.hazhar.com or email hassan.azhar@brisknest.us.
