Honey Hollow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Honey Hollow ("the app") is a match-3 puzzle game made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device: levels reached, stars, best scores, coins, boosters, daily-gift streak and settings. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Online leaderboard (optional)

If you choose to join the "Top Bees" leaderboard, the app sends the following to our leaderboard database (Google Firebase, operated by Google on our behalf):

  • the nickname you type in (please don't use your real name),
  • your highest level reached and total stars,
  • a random, anonymous player ID created by Firebase Authentication so only your device can update your entry.

Your nickname, level and stars are shown to other players on the leaderboard. We do not collect your email, real name, contacts or location, and we do not sell this information. It is used only for the leaderboards and team features.

You can delete your leaderboard entry at any time in the app: open the leaderboard and tap "Remove me from the leaderboard". You can also ask us to delete it through the contact link below. If you never join, nothing is sent.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (coins, boosters and the Golden Bee Pass) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Online features (optional)

The Hive Cup, Hives, the weekend Honey Harvest and the Top Bees leaderboard use the same nickname and anonymous player ID described above. For these the app also sends your weekly wins, your hive membership, the help you ask for or send, and how many honey pots you collected during the weekend event. Hives have no chat.

Invite codes: your 6-letter invite code is made from your anonymous player ID. When a friend uses your code, their anonymous ID and nickname are stored with your code so both of you can get the coin reward.

Save codes: if you choose "Move progress to another phone", the app uploads your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, settings) under a secret 10-letter code. Anyone who has that code can load that progress, so keep it private. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Ads

After your first 7 days, the app may show a short ad between some levels (at most every third finished level). You can remove these ads for good with a one-time purchase. Separately, you can choose to watch a short video for a reward (for example +5 moves, a heart, a free booster or a doubled daily gift). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad and your device, as described in Google's advertising policy. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report (the error, device model, Android version and app version) to Firebase Crashlytics, operated by Google, so we can fix the problem. Crash reports do not include your nickname or game progress.

Level statistics

To make levels fair (not too easy, not too hard), the app sends simple game events to Google Analytics for Firebase: which level you started, whether you won, lost or left it, how many moves you used and had left, and which in-app item was bought. These events are linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name, nickname or advertising ID, and are used only to improve the game. The app also downloads settings from Firebase Remote Config (such as how often ads appear or prize sizes) so we can adjust them without an update.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will update this policy and the date above before those changes are released.
