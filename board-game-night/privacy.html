Online play

When you use Quick Match or Play with Friends, the app signs in to Google Firebase with an anonymous account (no email, phone number or password). Firebase stores your table: the game, the display name and avatar you chose, the moves and the emotes. There is no free-text chat. Other players at your table see your display name and avatar. A table is deleted from the server shortly after its game ends; unfinished tables are deleted after one day. We use it for nothing else. Firebase is operated by Google (Firebase privacy).

Nearby play

Two-phone play uses Google Nearby Connections over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Android asks for "Nearby devices" permission (and Location on Android 12 and older, which Android requires for Bluetooth scanning). The game does not use or store your location. Messages go directly between the phones at the table.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: none during the first 7 days, never during play, and only between games. "Remove Ads" turns off full-screen ads. AdMob may use device identifiers such as the advertising ID to show and measure ads. Players in the EEA, UK and some US states are asked for consent first and can change their choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation so we can deliver what you bought.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Don't use online play, and no data is sent to Firebase.
  • Clear the game's data any time in Android Settings › Apps › Board Game Night › Storage.
  • Delete your online data yourself: see below.

Delete your online data

In the app, open Settings › Delete my online data and tap Delete. This removes, right away, everything the game stored about you on our Firebase server: your online-now mark, the tables you host, your display name and avatar at other tables, any open Quick Match request, and the anonymous online account itself. Your coins, items and progress stay on your phone. Moves at other people's tables carry no name and are deleted with those tables (within one day).

If you no longer have the app, email hassan.azhar@brisknest.us with the subject "Board Game Night data deletion"; we delete online data on request within 30 days. Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Board Game Night › Clear storage, removes everything stored on your phone.
