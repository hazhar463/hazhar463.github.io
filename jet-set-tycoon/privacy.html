Jet Set Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Jet Set Tycoon ("the app") is a property-trading board game made by Hassan Azhar / BriskNest ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information on our own servers. The app has no accounts and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress on your device: player names, Miles, Lives, Lucky Rerolls, unlocked tokens, board themes and dice, achievements, statistics, settings, and any game in progress. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's saved progress to your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot see it.

The app also offers an optional "transfer code" that you can copy and paste yourself to move progress to another device. The code is only shared if you choose to share it.

Optional reward videos (ads)

The game never shows ads during play. If you choose to tap "Watch" in the shop to earn Miles, a short video is shown by Google AdMob. To show and measure that video, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play. Optional in-app purchases (packs of Miles, Lives and Lucky Rerolls) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A separate kids' edition, Jet Set Junior, has no ads or purchases.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as online play, we will update this policy and the date above before those features are released.
